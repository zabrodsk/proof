Report a problem

Include the page or feature, what you expected, what happened, the approximate time, and any visible task or error identifier. Include your account email only when it is needed to locate your account.

Share a short, redacted example if it helps. Do not email passwords, access tokens, API keys, confidential documents, or sensitive personal records. A screenshot can expose document text; check it before attaching it.

A check is incomplete or seems wrong

Open the finding and read the quoted evidence in the original source. Abstract-only, unavailable, or incompletely extracted sources may leave a claim unverified. Scanned PDFs may need external OCR permission. Some layouts, tables, or equations may not extract correctly.

Report the finding identifier and a redacted example. Do not accept a wording change until you have reviewed it. An uncertain result is not proof that a claim is false.

Disconnect a connector

Open Proof's integrations workspace and revoke the platform grant. You can also disconnect in the assistant platform. Proof revocation cancels associated checks; imports already underway may finish. Previously shared results and saved sources remain until separately deleted.

Request deletion or stop waitlist emails

Email the support address with the subject "Proof account deletion" or "Proof waitlist removal". Include the account or waitlist email and specify what you want removed. Never include a password or token.

Library source deletion and document archiving do different things. Source deletion queues file cleanup and removes related records. Archiving keeps the document stored. Clearing browser data or signing out does not delete server data.

The manual account-deletion and waitlist-removal procedures require operator approval before these pages go live.
