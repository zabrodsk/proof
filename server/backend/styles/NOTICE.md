# Citation style attribution

The unmodified MLA Handbook 9th edition CSL style and US English locale are from the Citation Style Language project. Both are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Authors and contributors are retained in each XML file. `manifest.json` records the immutable source commits, URLs, and SHA-256 hashes.

The MLA style credits Sebastian Karcher and Andrew Dunning, with Patrick O'Brien as contributor. The locale credits Andrew Dunning, Sebastian Karcher, Rintze M. Zelle, Denis Meier, and Brenton M. Wiernik.

Class requirements are applied explicitly at runtime. The vendored source files are not edited. Formatting uses citeproc-js, pinned as `citeproc` 2.4.63. Its license and copyright notice are distributed in `node_modules/citeproc/LICENSE`.
